how it works
Tap a cell to place a note, tap it again to clear; drag to paint. One note per step in each lane. Rows are scale degrees, so the pattern stays in key when the mood changes. Drum cells are sixteenths: a tap cycles hit, accent, ghost, rest. Alt-click or shift-click a cell to hear it without placing it. Space plays and stops on a keyboard, and r goes back to the first step. Edits are heard the next time the playhead passes them. Each lane's strip follows the playhead: ticks are note starts, over the lane's level before the echo; the last pass stays dim. The mix button shows all four on one strip, their colours added. A bass voice plays at the level of the pluck it replaces.
