UNDERTONE

Generative game music in Go, played in the browser through an AudioWorklet. Tap to start audio. Headphones help with the low end.

gridA step sequencer on the engine: lead, bass and pulse lanes over the generated bed. A link carries the pattern. About 5 MB. grid + lgThe same grid with a mutate button whose variations are written in let-go. About 28 MB, most of it let-go's runtime. playerThe engine alone, generative, with a four-band meter. Scenes for dungeon, overworld, meadow, aurora and village at several levels. About 3 MB. descentlet-go driving the engine: lg code turns a scripted descent and fight into music parameters and sound cues. About 29 MB. designerShape a mood and hear it as you go: tempo, key, each layer's line across levels, drums. A mix mutes layers one at a time, and the result exports as an EDN mood for let-go. About 5 MB.
